- [ ] Add a fixed behavioral-evaluation adapter for the canonical audit result;

      or target semantics.

      late-only, duplicate, unsafe-mutation, platform-gap, local/CI-drift,

      target or authority forgery, material limits, and omission of unrelated
      contextual defects.

      subsystem delegation with lead-owned final calibration.

      assertions, intended fixes, or earlier agent outputs.

      digests without retaining raw transcripts or reasoning.

      audit target, stops on incomplete evidence, maps essential versus advisory

      authority.

      graders, fixtures, and simulated or recorded canonical results.
