# Eval suite audit review guidance

- Treat suite/evidence binding, complete producer-contract validation,
  committed definition/fixture checks, output creation, and canonical
  derivation as trust boundaries.
- Reject any path where age, passing history, or lack of recent failures can
  independently authorize retirement.
- Verify that coverage loss, demotion, changed behavior, and material cost
  increase stop for a decision.
- Verify standalone operation and consumer validation without repository-only
  imports.
