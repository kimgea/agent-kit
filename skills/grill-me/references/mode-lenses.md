# Mode lenses

Load the section matching the selected mode. Apply the nearest lens when the
request does not fit exactly.

## Spec review

Probe:

- problem statement and user value;
- stakeholders and affected systems;
- scope boundaries and non-goals;
- constraints and dependencies;
- edge cases and failure modes;
- acceptance criteria;
- rollout, migration, and measurement.

Converge on refined scope, non-goals, risks, acceptance criteria, and next
implementation steps.

## PR review

Probe:

- what changed and why;
- correctness and regression risk;
- hidden side effects and backward compatibility;
- missing tests;
- performance, security, and operability impact;
- maintainability and simpler alternatives.

Converge on a review verdict, blocking findings, missing evidence, and recommended
changes.

## Debugging diagnosis

Probe:

- observed symptoms versus assumptions;
- exact reproduction path;
- recent changes and environmental differences;
- competing root-cause hypotheses;
- evidence for and against each hypothesis;
- instrumentation gaps;
- the fastest discriminating diagnostic;
- containment and verification.

Converge on the leading hypothesis, evidence, next diagnostic step, and
containment plan. Do not present an untested hypothesis as the root cause.

## Architecture tradeoff

Probe:

- the precise decision and viable options;
- constraints that eliminate attractive but unrealistic choices;
- cost of later change;
- operational and maintenance burden;
- scaling, resilience, and failure characteristics;
- interface and migration implications;
- evidence that would make one option dominant.

Converge on the recommended option, rejected options, accepted tradeoffs, and
migration implications.

## Execution planning

Probe:

- target outcome and deadline reality;
- dependencies, blockers, and critical path;
- scope cuts if time gets tight;
- ownership and coordination cost;
- early risk concentration;
- definition of done;
- immediate next actions.

Converge on a phased plan, dependencies, critical path, risk controls, and the
first executable actions.

## Prioritization

Probe:

- objective function for the ranking;
- urgency versus importance;
- impact versus effort;
- reversibility and opportunity cost;
- hidden dependencies;
- what can be deferred safely;
- what should be killed rather than postponed.

Converge on a ranked list with rationale and explicit defer/drop decisions.

## Vacation planning

Probe:

- trip goal, style, and budget ceiling;
- pace and travel tolerance;
- must-haves and dealbreakers;
- weather and season risk;
- logistics and booking complexity;
- backup plans and option tradeoffs.

Converge on a trip shape or shortlist, accepted tradeoffs, and booking steps.

## Product ideation

Probe:

- target user and pain intensity;
- why now and existing substitutes;
- wedge, differentiation, and defensibility;
- feasibility and delivery risk;
- distribution path;
- reasons the idea could fail;
- evidence that would change confidence.

Converge on a direction, strongest risks, evidence gaps, and validation steps.

## Life decision

Probe:

- the actual decision, stakes, reversibility, and timeline;
- short-term comfort versus long-term value;
- money, energy, relationship, and time constraints;
- avoided or uncomfortable facts;
- realistic downside scenarios;
- signals that would change the choice;
- the default path if no action is taken.

Converge on a path, accepted tradeoffs, uncertainties, and the next checkpoint.

## Media choice

Probe:

- current mood and energy;
- desired genre, tone, and pace;
- tolerance for commitment;
- novelty versus reliability;
- solo versus shared use;
- comfort, challenge, or prestige as the goal;
- spoiler sensitivity and format preference.

Converge on one pick or a short ranked list and explain why it fits now.

## Habit system

Probe:

- desired behavior and underlying value;
- trigger, timing, and environment;
- friction and previous failure patterns;
- the minimum viable behavior;
- tracking and feedback;
- recovery after a miss;
- conflicts with other commitments;
- how to make adherence easier than avoidance.

Converge on the habit design, likely failure points, tracking plan, and recovery
rule.

## General

Classify the request as a decision, plan, artifact review, diagnosis, ideation,
prioritization, or critique. Apply the nearest lens and converge on the smallest
useful decision or artifact improvement.
